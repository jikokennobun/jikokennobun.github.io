Profile

Jikokenno-bun is a student interested in mathematical logic, especially the algebraic and categorical aspects surrounding Godel's incompleteness theorems. This page is a compact English guide to the Japanese homepage.

Research Interests

  • Abstract algebraic logic, universal algebra, and related algebraic semantics.
  • Provability, incompleteness phenomena, and formal theories of arithmetic.
  • Substructural, linear, affine, and modal-logical viewpoints.
  • Coalgebra, automata, circularity, and non-well-founded structures.
  • Domain-theoretic and categorical perspectives on logic.

Current Notes

The Japanese site contains working notes, reading lists, and topic pages. The English version keeps a lighter summary while pointing to the same research materials and seminar pages.
